    Thunder

    (Plural: thunder) is the sound produced alongside lightning. The sound of thunder varies from a direct crack to a low rumble, and its intensity depends on the listener's distance from the source. The sound of thunder is also called a rumble.

    Cause of Thunder

    Thunder occurs physically as a result of a sudden increase in pressure and temperature in the surrounding air caused by lightning. This expansion, in turn, creates shock waves that manifest as the sound of thunder.

    Other names

    Ar-Ra'd is the name of a surah in the Quran, and it is the thirteenth surah in order. It has forty-three verses, and it is a Medinan surah. Thunder is mentioned in verse 13 of Surah Ar-Ra'd, which is located in the thirteenth part of the Quran.

    Spring

    Spring is one of the four seasons of the year and is characterized by greenery, the growth and blooming of colorful flowers, and mild weather or temperatures compared to winter. Temperatures are also slightly lower compared to summer. Many tourist activities can be done during spring, which some describe as a season for connecting with nature and enjoying its natural beauty.

    Springtime

    Astronomically, spring begins on March 21st in the Northern Hemisphere and on September 22nd in the Southern Hemisphere. It continues until summer solstice, which begins on June 22nd in the Northern Hemisphere and on December 22nd in the Southern Hemisphere.

    Benefits of Spring

    Spring is considered a transitional phase between winter and summer, leading to climatic balance and improved conditions for the Earth.

    Autumn

    Autumn is one of the four seasons of the year and comes after summer and is followed by winter. In the cold polar regions, autumn is very short, and temperatures begin to gradually decrease until they reach the middle between the heat of summer and the cold of winter. Autumn is characterized by the falling of leaves from the trees.

    Characteristics of Autumn


    - The leaves change color from green to yellow, red, orange, or brown. This is the final stage before all the leaves fall from the tree.

    - The length of day and night are equal, after which the days gradually shorten and the nights gradually lengthen.

    - Autumn has a changeable weather pattern, combining elements of the other three seasons into one. The weather is generally mild, but may be punctuated by heat waves and cool winds, especially during the night and early morning.

    Information about summer:

    - The word "summer" is derived from the English word "summer," which itself has Indo-European origins.

    - Ancient pagans believed that lighting fires during summer festivals increased the sun's power, thus stimulating crop growth.

    - Ancient civilizations, such as the Romans, Greeks, and Chinese, celebrated the arrival of summer with their gods.

    The Sioux people celebrated summer with vibrantly colored dances representing the universe: red symbolizing sunset, blue the sky, yellow light, and black night.

    The date of summer

    Summer begins in the last third of June and lasts for three months, ending in the last third of September. The weather in summer is characterized by high temperatures, dryness, and cold storms. Summer is considered the season of celebrations and the favorite season for most peoples since ancient times until now.
